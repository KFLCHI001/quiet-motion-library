# Style menu

Offer a short menu before generating, not an open question. Show the few that suit the brief first, with a route hint (vector styles suit Lottie; rendered or textured styles suit video), then offer the full list. Add the chosen style's descriptor to every prompt.

| Group | Styles (descriptor to append) |
| --- | --- |
| Classic | Cartoon (bold outlines, bright color) · Flat (clean geometric shapes, minimal shading) · Anime (expressive large eyes, cel shading) · Comic (inked lines, halftone shading) · Pastel pop (soft gradients, rounded forms, gentle shadows) |
| Cute | Kawaii (pastel, tiny features) · Cute (soft, friendly, round) · Chibi (tiny body, oversized head) · Bubbly (puffy shapes, candy colors) · Blob (gloopy body, dot eyes) · Sticker (die-cut, thick white border) |
| Retro | Pixel art (8-bit) · Retro pop (1960s print colors) · Vaporwave (80s pastel neon) · Arcade (16-bit neon on dark) |
| 3D | 3D render (soft studio light) · Clay (handmade claymation, matte) · Isometric (30° geometric 3D) · Low poly (faceted surfaces) · Glass (frosted, translucent layers) |
| Painterly | Sketch (pencil) · Watercolor (soft washes) · Oil (visible brushstrokes) · Gouache (opaque matte, picture-book) · Ink wash (flowing black ink) · Woodcut (carved lines, print texture) |
| Graphic | Pop art (Ben-Day dots) · Graffiti (spray paint) · Neon (glowing outlines on dark) · Street art (stencil layers) |
| Minimal | Line art (single-weight lines) · Monoline (one continuous line) · Geometric (basic shapes) · Silhouette (solid fill) |
| Craft | Embroidery (stitched thread) · Paper cut (layered paper with shadow) · Felt (fuzzy, stitched edges) · Crayon (waxy strokes) |

Tailor the shortlist to the brief. For a calm, reflective product, lead with clay, gouache, watercolor, flat and cute, and hold back neon, graffiti and arcade.
